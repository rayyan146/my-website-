# 📚 Study Library

A modern, responsive subject-wise video lecture library to organize your study subjects and YouTube lectures with instant redirection, video thumbnail previews, and persistent local storage.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Subject Management**:
  - Add, edit, and delete subjects at any time.
  - Customize accent color badges for quick visual distinction.
  - Add optional descriptions or syllabus goals.

- **YouTube Link Management**:
  - Add video lecture links either using the top **Quick Add bar** or directly inside each subject card.
  - Supports standard YouTube links (`watch?v=...`), short links (`youtu.be/...`), YouTube Shorts, and embeds.
  - Automatic YouTube video thumbnail extraction with fallback.
  - Edit or delete links with one click.

- **Instant Redirection**:
  - Clicking any video title or the **Watch** button immediately redirects you to YouTube in a new tab.
  - Click on the thumbnail to open a quick modal preview player with embedded playback.

- **Automatic Persistence**:
  - All subjects and links are saved to your browser's `localStorage`. Next time you open the website, all your data will be right there.
  - Includes **Export / Import JSON** backup options to save or transfer your bookmarks.

- **Search & Filters**:
  - Real-time instant search across subjects, video titles, notes, and URLs.
  - Quick subject filter chips to focus on one subject at a time.
